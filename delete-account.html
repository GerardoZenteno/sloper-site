ACCOUNT MANAGEMENT

Delete Your Bloxfit Account

Bloxfit is developed by Zega Apps.

Last Updated: July 23, 2026

You can request deletion of your Bloxfit account and all associated data at any time, whether or not you currently have the app installed.

Deletion is permanent, immediate, and cannot be undone.

smartphone Option 1: Delete your account from within the app (recommended)

  1. Open the Bloxfit app and log in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the action when prompted.
Immediate Effect: Your account and all associated data are deleted immediately upon confirmation.

mail Option 2: Request deletion by email (if you no longer have the app installed)

Send an email to zega.apps.dev@gmail.com from the email address associated with your Bloxfit account, with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".

Please include the email address linked to your Bloxfit account (or your Roblox username, if you signed up via Google) so we can locate and verify your account. We will process your request and delete your account and data within 5 business days, and will confirm by email once completed.

delete_forever What data is deleted

When your account is deleted (via the app or by email request), the following data is permanently removed from our systems:

Data Deleted?
Email address and authentication credentials (Firebase Auth / Google Sign-In) Yes, immediately
Display name / nickname, Roblox username, country, profile picture Yes, immediately
Bloxcoins balance and transaction history Yes, immediately
Chest and check-in history Yes, immediately
Boost leaderboard entries (username, country flag, selected goal) Yes, immediately — removed from all public leaderboards
Uploaded outfit images Not applicable — never stored on our servers to begin with

shield Data that may be retained

We do not retain personally identifiable data after account deletion. We may retain anonymized, aggregated usage data (which cannot be linked back to you) for analytics purposes, as described in our Privacy Policy. Third-party services we use (such as Google Play Billing, for purchase records) may retain transaction data separately, in accordance with their own retention policies and applicable law (e.g., financial record-keeping requirements).
